AI agents make shipping features almost free. That's a problem. When the cost of building drops, the temptation is to ship everything, and users drown. This is about how to manage feature velocity so the product gets better instead of louder.

## Outline

- The economics shift: build cost is collapsing, attention cost isn't.
- What feature slop looks like in practice (release notes, surface area, settings pages).
- User burndown: the pattern of users disengaging when products keep changing under them.
- Heuristics for what to *not* ship even when you can.
- Process: roadmap pruning, deprecation discipline, "feature debt" as a tracked metric.
