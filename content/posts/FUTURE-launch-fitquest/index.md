FitQuest turns the metrics from every device you already own into quests, stages and streaks. This is the launch post.

## Outline

- What it is, in one paragraph, before any of the why.
- The bet: the data was never the problem. Every device already tracks everything. What's missing is a reason to care about it on day forty.
- Why aggregation across devices is the hard part, and why most trackers don't do it.
- The quest mechanic, and how it got tested. A throwaway SwiftUI proof-of-concept against real HealthKit data, built to answer one question and then discarded, with the learnings going back into the docs.
- The line I won't cross: gamification that manipulates rather than motivates. Worth being specific, since this is where the genre usually rots.
- Who it's for and what it costs.
- What's next.
