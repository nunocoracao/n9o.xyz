Anima is a personal agent: one persistent agent per person, that remembers. This is the launch post.

## Outline

- What it is, in one paragraph, before any of the why.
- The bet: persistence and memory are the product. Everything else is a chat interface people already have.
- What made it hard. The honest version, not the highlight reel.
- The docs-first build. Anima had a `docs/` directory, a decision log and a Definition before it had a product codebase, and prototypes had to prove things before code existed. Link the Watchfire post rather than re-explaining the machinery.
- The hatch: the onboarding as a statement of what the thing is. Six questions that either shape the being or become its first memory.
- Who it's for and what it costs.
- What's next, and what I'm deliberately not building.
