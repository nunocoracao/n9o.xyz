How AI coding agents are collapsing the gap between idea and ship, and what that does to the traditional roles — PM, designer, engineer, QA, ops. What disappears, what gets harder, and what new shape the team takes.

## Outline

- The old loop: spec → ticket → engineer → review → QA → release.
- The new loop: spec → agent → review → ship.
- Where the bottlenecks moved (playtesting, review, judgment, taste).
- What each role is actually doing now.
- What this means for hiring, team size, and what "senior" looks like.
